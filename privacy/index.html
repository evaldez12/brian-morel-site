What we collect

How we use it

Text messages

If you check the box to receive text messages, we will text you about your request, appointments, and your account. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Our customer management and messaging platform (to store your request and send messages).
  • Our website host.
  • Advertising platforms such as Meta, only as measurement data when you take an action on the site, and only if their tools are enabled.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Cookies and ad tracking

We may use the Meta Pixel and similar tools to measure our ads. You can limit ad tracking in your browser settings and in your Facebook and Instagram ad preferences.

How long we keep it

We keep lead information for as long as it's useful to follow up, and client records for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, unless we must keep it by law.

Your choices

  • Use the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Contact us to see, correct, or delete your information.
